Chapter 1 · Sample collection

How does the sample stay chemically pristine?

Without strict rules, bacteria and temperature shifts destroy the sample before testing even begins. Ten steps keep the water exactly as collected until it reaches the lab.

Chapter 2 · Test A

Is nitrite present?

Qualitative detection with the sulphanilic acid + alpha-naphthylamine method. A pink azo dye forms only when nitrite is in the water.

NO₂⁻Nitrite from the sample, in acid
Diazonium saltForms with sulphanilic acid
+ alpha-naphthylamineCouples with the diazonium salt
Pink azo dyePink product: nitrite present
Chapter 3 · Test B

How much nitrite is there?

Quantitative estimation with the zinc iodide starch method. The faster the blue color appears, the higher the nitrite concentration.

NO₂⁻ + I⁻ + H⁺Nitrite meets iodide in acid
I₂Nitrite oxidizes iodide to iodine
+ starchIodine binds to the starch
Blue complexFaster blue = more nitrite
2 NO₂⁻ + 2 I⁻ + 4 H⁺ → 2 NO + I₂ + 2 H₂O
Blue directly0.50 mg/L
Blue after 30 s0.30 mg/L
Blue after 1 min0.15 mg/L
Blue after 3 min0.10 mg/L
Blue after 10 min0.05 mg/L
